Privacy Policy

Application: Bowloon (com.bowloon.game)
Effective Date: November 5, 2026
Last Updated: November 5, 2026
Core Privacy Commitment: Bowloon is built with privacy-first architecture. We do not collect, harvest, monetize, sell, or share personal identification data, geolocation details, or device contacts. The game is fully functional offline.

1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game application Bowloon (Package ID: com.bowloon.game), available on Google Play. It outlines how we handle user data, local device storage, hardware permissions, and user privacy rights under applicable data protection regulations including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA).

2. Data We Do Not Collect

We respect your personal privacy. Unlike many ad-heavy or social applications, Bowloon does NOT collect, store, or transmit:

  • Personally Identifiable Information (PII): No names, email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses, or government IDs.
  • Account Credentials: No account registration, social login (Facebook, Google, Apple), or passwords.
  • Precise or Approximate Geolocation: No GPS, Wi-Fi tri-angulation, or cell tower location data.
  • Financial & Payment Data: No credit card numbers, billing addresses, or banking details.
  • Sensitive Device Content: No access to your photos, camera, microphone, contacts, call logs, SMS, or file system outside the application's isolated sandbox.
  • Biometric Data: No facial recognition or fingerprint data.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

To enable gameplay progression, achievements, and player preferences, Bowloon saves certain non-identifying game variables strictly within your device's isolated local application storage (using standard Android encrypted sandbox storage and AsyncStorage). This data never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any remote server:

Data Category Examples Purpose Storage Location
Gameplay Progression Levels completed, stars earned (0–3), best scores per level Save progress across play sessions Local Device Only
Virtual Economy In-game coins balance, unlocked bows, equipped cosmetic items In-game item unlocks Local Device Only
User Preferences Music volume, SFX volume, haptic feedback toggle, selected theme Preserve customized settings Local Device Only

4. Device Hardware Permissions & Capabilities

Bowloon requests and uses only the minimum necessary standard Android runtime features required to render the physics and audiovisual gameplay experience:

  • Vibration / Haptic Feedback (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used exclusively to deliver tactile physical responses when stretching the bowstring, firing arrows, popping balloons, or completing levels. You can disable this feature at any time in the in-game Settings menu.
  • Audio Output: Used exclusively to play sound effects and background musical tracks through your device speakers or headphones.
  • Local Notifications (Optional): Used to schedule periodic local reminder notifications (such as daily level challenges). These are generated entirely on-device; no remote push token or network identifier is transmitted.

5. Advertisements & Third-Party SDKs

Bowloon integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display optional rewarded video advertisements (for in-game bonuses such as extra arrows or coins) and occasional non-intrusive interstitial ads between levels:

  • Data Processed by Advertising Partners: Google AdMob may process non-sensitive device and interaction information, including your device's Android Advertising ID (AAID), coarse location data derived from general IP ranges, app diagnostics, and ad interaction events (impressions, clicks) to serve ads, enforce frequency capping, and detect fraudulent activity.
  • Family & Children Protection: Ads are configured to request non-personalized, family-safe ad inventory compliant with Google Play Families Policy and COPPA standards.
  • Google Privacy Policy: For more details regarding how Google uses data in advertising, visit Google's Advertising Privacy & Terms.

Other core platform frameworks include:

  • Expo & React Native: Open-source application runtime engine.
  • Shopify React Native Skia: Hardware-accelerated 2D graphics rendering engine executed locally on device GPU.
  • Google Play Store Services: Standard Android operating system distribution mechanism for automatic application updates and integrity verification.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Global Standards)

Bowloon is designed as an accessible, family-friendly casual arcade game suitable for all audiences, including children under the age of 13:

  • We do not knowingly collect, request, or maintain any personal data from children.
  • We do not display behavioral, interest-based, or personalized advertisements.
  • We do not incorporate public chat rooms, messaging features, or social forums where children could share personal data.

If a parent or guardian believes their child has somehow provided personal data, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt steps to verify and ensure the data is cleared.

7. User Control & Data Deletion Rights

Because all game data resides exclusively on your local device, you possess total control over your information at all times:

  • To Clear All Data: Open your Android device Settings > Apps > Bowloon > Storage & Cache > Clear Storage (or Clear Data). This will permanently reset all game progression, unlocked bows, coins, and high scores.
  • To Delete All Data: Uninstalling the Bowloon application from your device instantly removes the application sandbox, including all saved game states and preference files.

8. Compliance with International Privacy Regulations

8.1 European Union / United Kingdom (GDPR / UK GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation, the lawful basis for processing local game data is our legitimate interest in delivering the requested interactive entertainment application. Because we do not store personal data on remote servers, your rights (access, rectification, erasure, and data portability) are fully exercised directly on your device via the data deletion steps outlined above.

8.2 California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act and California Privacy Rights Act:

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • We do not collect sensitive personal information as defined by the CPRA.

9. Data Security

We maintain data security by ensuring no personal data is collected or transmitted across the public internet. Local game files are stored within the secure, sandboxed application directory provided by the Android operating system, preventing unauthorized access by other installed applications.
